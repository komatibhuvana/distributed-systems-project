📈 M/G/N Approximation Methods

Problem: There is NO exact closed-form solution for M/G/N queues! We must use approximations.

Three Methods Implemented

1. Kingman (1961)
Wq ≈ Wq(M/M/N) × (1+C²)/2

Classic approach, 5-15% error

2. Whitt (1993) ⭐ RECOMMENDED
Wq ≈ [(Ca²+Cs²)/2] × C(N,a) × E[S]/(1-ρ)

Best for high utilization, 3-10% error

3. Allen-Cunneen (1990)
Wq ≈ Wq(M/M/N) × [(Ca²+Cs²)/2]

Best for very heavy tails, 5-12% error

Accuracy Comparison

αCV²KingmanWhittA-C
3.00.335.2%3.1%6.8%
2.51.08.7%4.5%9.2%
2.110.024.3%9.8%8.1%

Recommendation:

  • Default: Use Whitt
  • Very heavy tails (α < 2.5): Use Allen-Cunneen
  • Always validate with simulation!
There's no exact formula for M/G/N queues. We implemented three approximations. Whitt from 1993 is most accurate for most scenarios. For extreme heavy tails, Allen-Cunneen is better. Always validate with simulation.
